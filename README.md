# Thoughts

A public notebook for ideas I want to keep thinking about.

Mostly notes on research, markets, technology, books, and other questions that stay with me. Some entries may be short; some may turn into longer essays later.

These are working notes rather than finished essays.

## Notes

New entries will appear here as I add them.

## Structure

- `writing/` — longer reflections and essays
- `research/` — research questions, observations, and ideas
- `reading/` — notes from books and papers
- `misc/` — everything that does not fit neatly elsewhere
- `templates/` — lightweight templates for new entries
